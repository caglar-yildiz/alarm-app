Privacy Policy

Application: BENG Alarm

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

BENG Alarm ("the application") is an Android alarm clock. This policy explains what information the application handles, where it goes and why. In short: the application has no ads, no accounts and no analytics, and the developer does not receive or store any personal data.

2. Advertising, Accounts and Analytics

The application does not display advertisements, does not use the advertising ID, does not include analytics or tracking tools, and does not require users to create an account or sign in.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Alarms, timers, settings, the chosen language, the chosen weather location, the free-trial record and your wake-up statistics (when each alarm was turned off, snoozed or missed) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

4. Android Backup and Google Block Store

If Android backup is turned on, the data in section 3 is included in your device's backup to your Google account so it can be restored after reinstalling the application or moving to a new phone. The start date of the free trial, together with a one-way hash of the device's Android ID, is also kept in Google Block Store so the 7-day free trial can be used only once per phone and Google account. These backups are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, are end-to-end encrypted where supported by your device, and cannot be read by the developer. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

5. Weather

When you use the weather feature, the application sends the latitude and longitude of the province or district you selected to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no) over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to retrieve a forecast. The application does not use GPS or your device's location services; only the place you choose is sent. The request also includes the application's name and version. MET Norway receives these requests under its own privacy terms. If you do not use the weather feature, no request is made.

6. Camera

The camera is used only for the optional QR-code mission that dismisses an alarm. Camera images are processed on your device to read the code; they are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

6a. Step Counting

The optional walking mission uses your phone's step sensor, with the Physical activity permission, to count steps while the alarm screen is open. Steps are counted on your device only; they are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

6b. Reading the Briefing Aloud

If you turn on "Read it aloud", the morning briefing text (greeting, time, weather and streak) is passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, such as Google's speech services, which speaks it. The developer does not receive this text.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

BENG Premium is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; the developer does not receive your payment details. The application asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription and keeps only that result on your device. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store.

8. Application Permissions

The application requests these Android permissions:

  • Exact alarms: to ring alarms exactly on time.
  • Notifications: to show ringing alarms, upcoming alarms, finished timers, the wake check and bedtime reminders.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show a ringing alarm on the lock screen.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep playing the alarm sound.
  • Run at startup: to restore scheduled alarms after the device restarts.
  • Camera: for the optional QR-code mission (see section 6).
  • Physical activity: for the optional walking mission (see section 6a).
  • Internet: for the weather feature and Google Play subscriptions.

9. Data Sharing and Sale

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal data. Data is sent to third parties only as described in sections 4, 5 and 7, when you use those features.

10. Data Deletion

Because the developer does not store your data, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. You can delete all application data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling it, and remove backups from your Google account settings.

11. Children's Privacy

The application is a general-purpose alarm clock intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer using the contact information provided in the application's Google Play Store listing.
